KEY NUMBERS
HD 189733 b: 63 light-years away, 2.2-day orbit, ~1,700°F, winds up to ~5,400 mph (about 7× the speed of sound)
TrES-2b: reflects less than 1% of the light that hits it
WASP-76b: dayside above 2,400°C (4,300°F), iron vapor that rains out on the night side
KELT-9b: dayside ~4,600 K (~7,800°F), hotter than most red dwarf stars
WASP-12b: ~1-day orbit, stretched into an egg; estimated ~3–10 million years left
TOI-849b: Neptune-sized but ~40× Earth's mass, 18-hour orbit, in the "Neptune desert"
PSR J1719-1438 b: orbits a pulsar every 2 h 10 min, about Jupiter's mass but less than 4.5× Earth's width
PSO J318.5-22: ~80 light-years away, ~6× Jupiter's mass, drifting with no star
PSR B1620-26 b (Methuselah): ~12.7 billion years old, in globular cluster M4 (~7,200 light-years, updated estimate)
8 UMi b (Halla): orbits the red giant Baekdu at ~0.5 AU, inside the size its star should have swelled to
