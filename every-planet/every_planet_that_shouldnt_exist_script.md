Because this planet orbits its star every 2.2 days, one side is locked in permanent, blistering daylight. That extreme temperature gap whips up winds blowing up to 5,400 miles per hour. That's roughly seven times the speed of sound.

Halla is a Jupiter-like gas giant orbiting a star named Baekdu, over 500 light-years away, at just half the distance between Earth and the Sun.

Either way, it's a world that cheated cosmic death.
