08:07 Charged black holes & white holes
09:00 Wrap-up

KEY NUMBERS
Mount Everest as a black hole: radius ≈ 0.24 picometers
A black hole of ~230 metric tons (a blue whale) evaporates in about 1 second (Hawking radiation)
Cygnus X-1: ~7,000 light-years away, the first black hole we strongly confirmed
GW190521 (LIGO/Virgo): merger that left a black hole of ~142 solar masses
Sagittarius A*: ~4.3 million Suns, imaged by the Event Horizon Telescope in 2022
TON 618: up to ~66 billion Suns, event-horizon radius up to ~1,300 AU
Phoenix A: ~100 billion Suns (estimate)
Proposed growth limit for accreting black holes: ~50 billion Suns
