## Description
What if every oxygen atom on Earth vanished for just 5 seconds? The viral story says buildings turn to dust, oceans boil into space and everyone gets a sunburn. It gets three things wrong, and the real answer is even scarier.

Here's the experiment: every oxygen atom disappears at once, then snaps back at 5.00 seconds. We slow it down and follow the real physics and chemistry: the air pressure drop, the ozone layer, concrete and rock (oxygen is ~45% of Earth's crust), metals that cold weld (NASA's Galileo spacecraft, 1991), oceans that flash into hydrogen gas, and what happens when the oxygen comes back.
