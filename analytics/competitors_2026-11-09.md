# Competitor analysis — 2026-11-09 (vidIQ public data; no access to their CTR/retention)

## @OuranosSky — Shorts only
- 66 public shorts, 5.95 M views, 4,670 subs. Breakout started 2026-10-03/04 when uploads jumped from ~10 to 31 in 4 days;
  since then ~2–4 shorts per day. Almost every short 40k–510k views (top: "What If the Sun Turned Blue?" 511k).
- Length 22–50 s (most 25–35 s), 50–80 spoken words. Realistic 3D renders, one big object centred, big caption.
- Title = "What If [Sun/Earth/Moon/Jupiter] + ONE absurd change?" (turned blue, made of gold, a pyramid, spun the other
  way, Saturn never existed) or a surprising fact ("The Moon Is Rusting", "Earth Has a Second Moon").
- Script pattern: line 1 = the title + "you'd think X… but" → one physical reason → 2–3 escalating real numbers →
  punchline ending ("no Saturn, no Earth, no us — and no Saturday either"). No intro, no CTA. The short is complete.

## @lauralonglegs1 — Long only, ~10 min
- 5 videos, 81k views, 591 subs; "Every Type Of Star Explained in 10 Minutes" = 57.7k in 6 days,
  "What If We Replaced The Sun With Every Type Of Star?" = 15.8k in 3 days.
- Format = "Every type of X" list (12 items), flat clean drawings; thumbnail = 3×4 labelled grid + big title text.
- Hook = three extreme items in 10 s ("a star as cold as a winter day… one that would swallow Earth… one that doesn't
  exist yet") → "let's put every one where our Sun is and see what happens to YOU".
- Every section ends with a teaser for the next ("The next one is the hottest…") = built-in re-hook every ~40 s.
- Mid-video subscribe line (~6:30, before the "much worse" section), ending = comment question.
