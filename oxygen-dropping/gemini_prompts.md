# Gemini prompts: "What If Earth's Oxygen Kept Dropping and Couldn't Stop?"

## How to use these prompts
- Paste the **STYLE BASE** first, then the scene prompt.
- 16:9, the largest size Gemini gives.
- **No text, numbers or letters in any image.** Claude adds the counter, labels and numbers.
- **No character in the backgrounds.** The red-shirt character is generated separately (section C) and placed by Claude.
- **Continuity:** generate 01 first, then attach `01_street_morning.png` as a reference for 17 and T1 ("the same street as the attached image").
- **Keep the top-right corner calm** in every scene (sky, wall or plain area): the DAY / O₂ counter sits there.
- **Lighting runs from morning (day 1) to evening and night (days 12–16).** That shows time passing. Don't make the air look coloured or foggy: in this experiment the air looks exactly the same.

---

## STYLE BASE (paste before every prompt)

```
Hand-drawn ink illustration for an educational science YouTube channel. Clean, confident ink outlines in dark navy (#141824), slightly wobbly hand-drawn lines, flat colours with soft cel shading, simple shapes that stay readable on a phone screen. Cinematic eye-level composition, like a still from an animated film. Not photorealistic, not a 3D render, not anime. No text, no letters, no numbers, no signs with writing, no logos, no watermark. Keep the top-right corner of the image calm and simple. 16:9, highest resolution.
```
