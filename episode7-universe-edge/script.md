# Episode 7 — What If the Universe Has No Edge?  (script v2)

**Identity check:** one impossible scenario (you fly straight forever, on a ship that never stops) + the real physics
of what you would find. **Formula:** Hook → Setup/Promise → Science → Consequences (escalating) → Twist → callback + CTA.
Owner's lines kept word-for-word: the opening, "you see Earth. In front of you.", the ocean/horizon analogy,
"finite… and still have no edge" + the Earth walk, "Not a copy. Not a twin. You. From behind.",
"You are trapped in a bubble with no walls.", "So does the universe have an edge? No… no wall to find."
ElevenLabs v3, voice "Mark", Speed 1.1 for the hook. Hook tags = [excited]/[mischievously] (never [serious]).

## Voice script (paste as one file)

```
[excited] You're flying straight. No turning. No stopping.
You pass the Moon. The Sun. The last star of our galaxy.
You pass the farthest light any telescope has ever seen. Forty-six billion light-years from home.
And you keep going. And going.
And then... you see Earth. In front of you.
[mischievously] You never turned around.
So how did you get back?
Before this video is over, you'll see the back of your own head... and the one reason you can never, ever go home.

[curious] But first, one thing almost everyone gets wrong about the universe.
Everyone thinks we can see the whole universe. We can't.
We only see the observable universe. Forty-six billion light-years in every direction. Ninety-three billion across.
That's just the part where light has had time to reach us.
It's like standing in a dark ocean. You see to the horizon. But the ocean doesn't stop at the horizon.
So what's past the horizon? A wall? An edge?

[excited] Here's the mind-breaker. The universe can be finite... and still have no edge.
Think of Earth. Walk straight. You never fall off. You just... come back.
The surface is finite. But it has no edge.
Now do that in three dimensions. Like an old arcade game: fly off the right side of the screen... and you come back in on the left.
No wall. No edge. Space might simply... loop. That's how Earth ended up in front of you.

[mischievously] Remember my promise? Here it is.
If space loops, light loops too. Including the light bouncing off you.
So imagine the universe was small. You look out of the window, straight ahead... and someone is out there.
It's you. Not a copy. Not a twin. You. From behind.
Light from your own back went all the way around the universe... and came back into your eyes.
Look left. Right. Up. You, again and again. Your own past, lined up across the sky.

[serious] This isn't just a thought experiment. Scientists went looking.
They searched the oldest light in the universe for a ghost copy of our own sky. The same pattern, showing up twice.
They found... nothing.
So if space loops, the loop is almost as big as everything we can see. Or far bigger.

[curious] Fine. Then just fly farther. You've got forever, right?
[serious] No. The universe isn't standing still. It's expanding... faster and faster.
A beam of light leaving Earth tonight will never reach anything that's more than about sixteen billion light-years away today.
About ninety-five percent of the universe we can see is already out of reach. At any speed. Forever.
The road home stretches faster than you can travel.
You are trapped in a bubble with no walls.

So does the universe have an edge? No.
It might be infinite. It might be finite, and curved. But one thing is certain: there is no wall to find.
You can fly forever... and never hit a wall. And never make it home.
[excited] So. You're out there, alone... and you see the back of your own head. Do you wave... or do you run? Comment HI... or RUN!
If this blew your mind, hit like!
```
