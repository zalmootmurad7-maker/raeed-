THE NUMBERS
- Observable universe: ~46.5 billion light-years in every direction (~93 billion across)
- Space is flat to within ~0.2% (Planck 2018 + BAO) — and a flat universe can still loop (like a 3-torus)
- Matched-circle searches in the cosmic microwave background (WMAP, Planck) found no "ghost copy" of our sky
- Cosmic event horizon: ~16 billion light-years — light leaving Earth tonight never reaches anything farther
- So about 95% of the observable universe is already out of reach (1 − (16/46.5)³ ≈ 96% by volume)
